Our mission

Making therapy reach everyone

Physiotherapy works — but for many older adults, getting to a clinic regularly is hard. ViMove brings guided, AI-tracked exercise into the living room, so staying active doesn't depend on appointments, travel, or cost.

Accessible

No clinic, no app store, no special hardware. A browser and a webcam are all it takes.

Practical

Programs are built around real, repeatable movements that fit into an everyday routine.

Private

AI runs entirely in the browser. The camera feed stays on the device and is never uploaded.

The technology

Computer vision that understands movement

ViMove uses on-device machine-learning models to detect the landmarks of your hands, face, and full body in real time — over 500 points tracked every frame.

By measuring how those points move, ViMove recognizes each exercise, validates the motion, and counts a repetition only when it's done correctly. A short calibration step adapts the tracking to your body and your space, so it works for different heights, distances, and rooms.
