How it works

Therapy in three simple steps

No clinic visit, no special equipment, no technical know-how. If you can open a web page, you can use ViMove.

1

Tell us about you

Enter your age, gender, and condition. ViMove assembles an exercise program suited to your needs.

2

Turn on your camera

Allow camera access in your browser. Everything is processed locally — your video is never uploaded.

3

Move — we count

Follow each exercise on screen. AI tracks your motion, counts your reps, and moves you to the next one.

Why ViMove

Built for real people, real homes

Clinical-grade motion tracking made approachable enough for daily use by older adults and their caregivers.

AI motion tracking

Real-time hand, face, and full-body pose detection recognizes each movement and validates your form.

Personalized programs

Exercises are selected and dosed for your condition, then tracked rep by rep through the whole set.

Live feedback

An on-screen progress bar and live counter show exactly how you're doing as you move.

Private by design

All analysis runs in your browser. Your camera feed stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

Any device

Works on laptops, tablets, and phones with a camera. No app store, no installation, no setup.

Made for accessibility

Large type, high contrast, and big touch targets — designed first for older adults and those with tremor.
