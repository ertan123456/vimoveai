Privacy Policy

ViMove AI respects your privacy. Here is exactly what happens with your information — in plain language.

Your camera

AI movement analysis runs entirely inside your browser. If you use ViMove AI on your own, no video is recorded or sent anywhere. If your physiotherapist created your account, your exercise sessions are recorded and stored securely so that only your own specialist can review them to follow your progress — no one else can see them, and you can ask your specialist to delete them at any time.

Your results

If you use ViMove AI without signing in, your session results and progress stay only in your browser's local storage, on your device. If you sign in, your results are also stored securely in our database (hosted in the EU) so that you can see your progress on any device and only your own specialist can follow it. No one else can see them.

What we collect

We ask for your age, gender and condition only to build a suitable exercise program; this is used in your browser and is not stored on our servers together with your identity. We use anonymous visitor analytics (Google Analytics) to count visits — no names and no health data.

ViMove AI is a wellness and exercise tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not collect medical records. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any exercise program.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything stored with it at any time — see the Account & Data Deletion page.
